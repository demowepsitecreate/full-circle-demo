The core idea

Both sides matter.

A complete learning experience should examine both outcomes instead of looking only at the finish line.

SUCCESS PERSPECTIVE

“What helped me move forward?”

  • Which decisions worked?
  • Which habits made a difference?
  • What should students repeat?
  • What would I recommend now?
FAILURE PERSPECTIVE

“What should I have avoided?”

  • Where did things go wrong?
  • Which decisions created problems?
  • What should students avoid?
  • What did the experience teach me?
Complete knowledge comes from seeing the whole journey — not only the highlight reel.
